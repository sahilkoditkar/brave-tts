The Quiet Lighthouse

The lighthouse stood at the edge of the cliff. Its keeper, Dr. Mara Vale, climbed the stairs every night & lit the lamp.

Nobody remembered when the ships stopped coming. Still, she kept the light burning[1] for anyone who might be lost.
